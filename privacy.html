你輸入的所有內容（科目、項目、複習紀錄、設定）只儲存在你的裝置上，我們沒有伺服器，也不會上傳這些資料。

  • 免費版使用 Google Firebase Analytics 收集匿名的使用統計（見下方「使用統計」）；不使用其他分析或追蹤 SDK。
  • 通知權限只用於本機的每日提醒。
  • 「匯出」功能會把資料交給你選擇的分享對象（例如雲端硬碟）；那之後的處理由該服務的政策規範。
  • 解除安裝 app 即刪除所有資料。

廣告

免費版在畫面底部顯示一條橫幅廣告，並在完成一輪複習、按「完成」時偶爾顯示一則全螢幕廣告（每天最多一次），兩者都由 Google AdMob 提供。為了投放廣告，AdMob SDK 會向 Google 傳送你的裝置廣告 ID、IP 位址推算的概略位置、裝置型號與 app 版本等技術資訊；這些資料由 Google 依其隱私權政策處理，我們自己不會取得或儲存。你可以在裝置的「設定 › Google › 廣告」重設廣告 ID 或選擇退出個人化廣告。

歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士的使用者在首次啟動時會看到同意選項，之後可在 app 的「設定 › 廣告隱私設定」隨時修改。

複習中的畫面不顯示廣告。購買「完整版」後，app 不再載入廣告 SDK，也不再傳送上述資料。

使用統計

免費版使用 Google Firebase Analytics 收集匿名的使用統計，用來了解廣告與升級提示的安排是否讓人流失，並改善 app。會收集：app 的啟動與使用時間、完成複習的日數、是否出現全螢幕廣告與升級畫面（及出現的原因）、裝置型號、作業系統與 app 版本、由 IP 位址推算的概略位置，以及 Firebase 為 app 安裝產生的識別碼與裝置廣告 ID。不包含你輸入的科目、項目或複習內容。這些資料由 Google 依其隱私權政策處理。

免費版另使用 Firebase Remote Config 與 A/B 測試，隨機決定部分使用者是否會看到全螢幕廣告，用來評估廣告對使用體驗的影響。這會用到 Firebase 為 app 安裝產生的識別碼，與使用統計同樣在同意之後才啟用。

歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士的使用者在同意選項處理完之前不會收集；其他地區在廣告功能初始化後開始收集。購買「完整版」後，app 會關閉使用統計；已收集的資料仍保留在 Google 的系統中。你也可以在裝置的「設定 › Google › 廣告」重設廣告 ID。

應用程式內購買

「完整版」是透過 Google Play 完成的一次性購買。付款資料由 Google Play 處理，我們不會取得你的付款資訊；購買紀錄綁定你的 Google 帳號，換裝置後可在「設定 › 還原購買」還原。退款依 Google Play 的退款政策辦理。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

Everything you enter (subjects, items, review history, settings) is stored only on your device. We run no servers and never upload this data.

  • The free version uses Google Firebase Analytics for anonymous usage statistics (see “Usage statistics” below); no other analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • The notification permission is used only for local daily reminders.
  • "Export" hands your data to the share target you choose (e.g. a cloud drive); from there the policy of that service applies.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all data.

Advertising

The free version shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen and, when you tap “Finish” after a review session, occasionally a full-screen ad (at most once a day). Both are served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, the AdMob SDK sends Google your device's advertising ID, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and technical details such as device model and app version. Google processes this data under its Privacy Policy; we never receive or store it. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device's Settings › Google › Ads.

Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent on first launch and can change their choice at any time in the app under Settings › Ad privacy options.

No ads are shown during a review session. After purchasing the Full version, the app no longer loads the ad SDK and no longer sends the data above.

Usage statistics

The free version uses Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics, to understand whether the placement of ads and upgrade prompts makes people stop using the app, and to improve it. It collects: app launches and usage time, the number of days on which you completed a review session, whether a full-screen ad or the upgrade screen was shown (and why), device model, operating system and app version, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and the identifier Firebase generates for each installation together with the device advertising ID. It does not include the subjects, items or review content you enter. Google processes this data under its Privacy Policy.

The free version also uses Firebase Remote Config and A/B Testing to randomly decide whether some users are shown full-screen ads, in order to evaluate how ads affect the experience. This uses the identifier Firebase generates for each installation and, like usage statistics, is enabled only after the consent step.

For users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, nothing is collected until the consent choice has been handled; elsewhere collection starts once the ad feature initialises. After you purchase the Full version the app turns usage statistics off; data already collected remains in Google's systems. You can also reset your advertising ID under your device's Settings › Google › Ads.

In-app purchase

The Full version is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google Play and never reach us. The purchase is tied to your Google account and can be restored on a new device under Settings › Restore purchase. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.
