Recall（複習曆）隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-09-07

Recall 是完全離線的 app。你輸入的所有內容（科目、項目、複習紀錄、設定）只儲存在你的裝置上。

  • 不收集任何個人資料，不上傳任何資料到伺服器。
  • 不使用分析、廣告或追蹤 SDK。
  • 通知權限只用於本機的每日提醒。
  • 「匯出」功能會把資料交給你選擇的分享對象（例如雲端硬碟）；那之後的處理由該服務的政策規範。
  • 解除安裝 app 即刪除所有資料。

Privacy Policy

Recall is a fully offline app. Everything you enter (subjects, items, review history, settings) is stored only on your device.

  • No personal data is collected and nothing is uploaded to any server.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • The notification permission is used only for local daily reminders.
  • "Export" hands your data to the share target you choose (e.g. a cloud drive); from there the policy of that service applies.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all data.
