Recall 複習曆

依遺忘曲線排複習日的行事曆 app：記下今天學了什麼（一章、一個問答、一批單字），複習日自動出現在行事曆上；複習時自評，間隔隨結果拉長或縮短。支援 FSRS 與固定間隔。資料只存在你的手機。

A calendar that schedules your reviews from the forgetting curve: log what you learned today, get review days automatically, rate each review and the intervals adapt. FSRS or fixed intervals. Your data stays on your phone.
